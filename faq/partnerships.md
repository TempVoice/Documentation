# Can my Discord partner with TempVoice for free premium?

Here's why:

* **Advertisement has no value to us.** We don't accept shoutouts or server promotion in exchange for Premium, it provides no meaningful benefit to the project.
* **We don't partner with any Discord servers**, regardless of size. This isn't a threshold you can reach, partnerships simply aren't something we offer.
* **If TempVoice is genuinely valuable to your server**, that's actually a reason to subscribe rather than a reason to expect it for free. A bot that provides real value to your community is worth supporting financially.

We know this isn't the answer everyone hopes for. In the past, we tried partnerships and they didn't work out well for us, we didn't see any real benefit, only downsides.

Because of that, we'd rather be upfront and say no from the start than lead anyone on. Thanks for understanding.
