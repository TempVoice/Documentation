# Creator Channels

> **Where do I find this?**
>
> Open the [Dashboard](https://tempvoice.xyz/dashboard) -> Select your Discord server -> `Creator Channels`

Each Creator Channel has its own configuration. Select one to open its settings.

## Add a Creator Channel

Click on `Add a new Creator!` and confirm with `Create`. This creates a new channel on your Discord server. You can also use [/setup](../../commands/setup.md) -> `New Creator`.

{% hint style="info" icon="crown" %}
The Free plan includes **2 Creator Channels**. [TempVoice Premium](https://tempvoice.xyz/premium) unlocks unlimited Creator Channels.

Servers that set up Creator Channels before December 1, 2025 can keep using up to **3** of those without Premium.
{% endhint %}

A Creator Channel can't be created if your server has reached [Discord's limit of 500 channels](../../faq/limits.md#discord-server).

## Delete a Creator Channel

Deleting a Creator Channel removes the channel from your Discord server and deletes all its settings. This can't be undone.

If you delete a Creator Channel directly in Discord, TempVoice removes its settings too.
